# Nara — Share Widget Spec

## App Identity
- **Name:** Nara
- **Tagline:** Every child is different. Now there's a guide for yours.
- **Language:** English (en)
- **Primary color:** #E8836A (warm terracotta)
- **Background:** #FAF7F2 (warm ivory)

## Share Scenario 1 — Child Milestone
**Trigger:** Parent taps "Log This Milestone" on the Progress & Patterns screen.
**Share card layout:**
- Top: Nara logo (N in terracotta circle)
- Headline: "[Child name] reached a milestone! 🎉"
- Body: The milestone text logged by the parent
- Footer: "Tracked with Nara · nara-039e736d.eazo.dev"
- Background: warm ivory with soft terracotta accent

## Share Scenario 2 — Weekly Progress
**Trigger:** Parent taps "Share Progress Report" on Progress screen.
**Share card layout:**
- Top: Nara logo
- Headline: "[Child name]'s Weekly Progress"
- Body: Key wins and improvements from the week
- Footer: "Powered by Nara"

## Share Scenario 3 — Crisis Tip
**Trigger:** Parent taps "Share This Tip" after a crisis mode session.
**Share card layout:**
- Top: Nara logo
- Headline: "What worked for [child name] today"
- Body: The do-this-now steps that helped
- Footer: "From Nara — your parenting companion"
